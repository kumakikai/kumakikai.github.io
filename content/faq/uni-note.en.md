## Basics

### What kind of app is Uni:Note?
It is a handwriting note app for iPad.  
It is designed around writing with Apple Pencil and organizing notes by subject.

### Which devices are supported?
iPad only.

### Is Apple Pencil required?
Handwriting is designed for Apple Pencil.  
Finger input is mainly used for scrolling and moving photos.

### Do I need to log in or create an account?
No.

---

## Notes and pages

### Is a note title required?
No.  
If you create a note with the title left blank, today's date is used as the note name.

### What is the title prompt shown when I first open a new subject?
It is the title of the first note.  
If you leave it blank and choose **OK** or **Start Writing**, that day's date becomes the title.

If you go back without confirming anything, the in-progress first note is not kept.

### Can I separate notes within the same subject?
Yes.  
You can add one from **New Note** in the note list.

### How are new pages added?
When you start writing on the last page, the next page is added automatically.

### Can I delete a page?
Yes.  
You can delete it by pressing and holding the target page in **Pages**.

However, if a note has only one page, the page itself is kept and only its content is cleared.

### Can I protect a note?
Yes.  
When you choose **Protect** in the note list, the note becomes read-only. You can also require biometrics to remove protection.

### Can I rename a subject?
Not at this time.  
Note titles can be changed from the note list.

### What can be restored from Trash?
Subjects and notes shown in the note list.  
Trash groups deleted items by subject, and tapping a subject shows the notes inside it.

Even if a subject is deleted, you can restore the whole subject or restore only individual notes.  
Deleted pages do not go to Trash and are removed immediately.

## Photos and PDFs

### Can I insert photos?
Yes.  
You can insert photos one at a time with **Attach Photo**. After inserting, you can move or resize them, and double-tap to **Lock Photo** or **Unlock Photo**.

### Can I insert PDFs?
Yes.  
You can choose **Attach PDF** and place one page from a PDF file onto the page you are currently viewing.

If the page already has a PDF, the app asks whether you want to replace it.

### Can I export PDFs?
Yes.  
You can choose **Export PDF** and export **this note only** or **the entire subject** as PDF.

## Data and storage

### Where is my data stored?
Inside the app on your device.

### Is automatic sync available?
Not at this time.  
If needed, please use Backup / Restore in Settings.

### Is backup available?
Yes.  
You can create a backup file from Settings and save it through the Files app. Restore is also available from Settings.

### Can I use the app immediately after restoring?
After restoring, close the app once and open it again.  
The next launch opens the Home screen.

---

## Limits and pricing

### Are there limits in the free version?
Yes.  
In the free version, you can create up to 5 subjects and up to 3 notes in each subject.

### What changes in the premium version?
The creation limits for subjects and notes are removed.

## Settings

### What settings are available?
You can mainly change the following:

- Default Template
- Screen opened at launch
- Home terminology (`Subject` / `Notebook` / `Group` / `Category`)
- Require Biometrics to Remove Protection
- Left-Handed Mode
- Header / footer visibility
- PDF Export
- Language

### Can I change the label shown as "Subject" on Home?
Yes.  
In Settings, you can choose **Subject / Notebook / Group / Category** for the label shown on Home.

### What can I change in Left-Handed Mode?
You can adjust the initial toolbar position, the red margin line position on ruled paper, and the PDF insertion position.

### Where can I open the How to Use page?
Open **How to Use** from **Support** in **Settings**.
