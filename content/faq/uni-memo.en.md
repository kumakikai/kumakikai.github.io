## Basics

### What kind of app is Uni:Memo?
Uni:Memo is a companion app for Uni:Note.  
It imports backups created in Uni:Note and lets you review them on iPhone in read-only mode.

### Can I use Uni:Memo by itself?
No.  
You need a backup file created in Uni:Note.

### Which devices are supported?
iPhone only.

### Do I need to log in or create an account?
No.

---

## Importing backups

### What can I import?
You can import backup zip files created in Uni:Note.

### Does importing change the original backup file?
No.  
The selected zip is validated and expanded inside the app, so the original file is not modified.

### What happens if import fails?
Damaged or invalid backups are stopped safely.  
If you already have an imported snapshot, Uni:Memo keeps it and shows the error instead of replacing it with broken data.

### Is automatic sync available?
Not at this time.  
Uni:Memo is designed around reviewing backup data.

## Viewing

### What can I do in the app?
The main functions are:

- browse notebook lists
- open notes and sections
- view pages
- show or hide memorization layers

### Can I edit notes or pages?
No.  
Uni:Memo is read-only.

### Can I create, delete, or reorder notes?
No.  
Creation and editing are meant to stay in Uni:Note.

### What is the memorization feature?
It lets you show or hide the page-level memorization layer while reviewing.  
Uni:Memo does not edit memorization content.

### Can I zoom pages?
Yes.  
You can zoom with pinch gestures or a double tap.

### Can I resume from the last place I was reading?
Yes.  
You can turn that behavior on or off in Settings.

## Settings

### What settings are available?
The main settings are:

- Language
- Theme
- Resume Last Location
- Prevent Side Drift
- Top-level name
