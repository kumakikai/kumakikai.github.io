---

Uni:Memo is a **read-only companion app for reviewing Uni:Note backups on iPhone**.  
It is not a writing app. Its job is to import backup data and let you review your notes safely.

Requirements:

- iPhone only
- a backup zip created in Uni:Note
- focused on viewing and memorization layers, not editing or sync

---

## 1. Prepare a backup in Uni:Note

First, create a backup file in Uni:Note and save it somewhere you can access from the Files app.

Uni:Memo cannot create notes by itself, so the content you want to review must already be included in the Uni:Note backup.

---

## 2. Import the backup

On the shelf screen, choose `Import` and select a Uni:Note backup zip.

- choose the zip
- let the app validate its contents
- if valid, load the snapshot

Damaged or invalid backups are stopped safely.

---

## 3. Review the shelf

After import, the shelf shows your top-level notebook groups.

- summaries for note and page counts
- shortcuts to recently viewed notes

If nothing has been imported yet, Uni:Memo shows the import prompt first.

---

## 4. Open notes and pages

Open a subject from the shelf, then move through notes and sections until you reach the page you want to read.

- open the note list
- choose the note you want
- review pages in a continuous vertical flow

Uni:Memo is read-only, so it does not rename notes or edit pages.

---

## 5. Review pages

In the page view, you can focus on reading the imported content.

- pinch to zoom in or out
- double tap to zoom
- move through pages with vertical scrolling

The app is designed to make review safe and simple, even for heavier notes.

---

## 6. Toggle memorization layers

Use the eye button on the right side to show or hide the page-level memorization layer.

- hide the layer and try to recall
- show it again to check yourself

Uni:Memo does not edit the memorization mask itself.

---

## 7. Adjust settings

In Settings, you can mainly change:

- Language
- Theme
- Resume Last Location
- Prevent Side Drift
- Top-level name

Adjust them to fit how you prefer to review notes.

---

## 8. Replace the snapshot with a newer backup

When you create a newer backup in Uni:Note, run `Import` again in Uni:Memo.

The app switches only after the new zip passes validation.
