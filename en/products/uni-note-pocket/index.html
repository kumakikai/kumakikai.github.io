Carry your iPad study notes on iPhone.

Uni:Note Pocket is a read-only companion for reviewing Uni:Note notes and practice sets on iPhone. Check your study material during a commute or before class when opening your iPad is inconvenient.

Start by importing a Uni:Note backup. Browse notes by subject, scroll through pages and check practice answers and explanations. Create and edit in Uni:Note; carry and read in Pocket.

Key features

  • Import a backup

    Load a Uni:Note backup ZIP or select an Easy Backup.

  • Read continuous pages

    Scroll vertically through a note and use pinch or double-tap zoom for handwritten details.

  • Search by title

    Find a note by its title or browse your subjects.

  • Review questions and markers

    Check imported answers and explanations, and tap sticky markers in your notes.

Begin with your Uni:Note backup.

Import a backup prepared in Uni:Note to read your notes and practice sets in Pocket. Import a newer backup when you want to see updated material.

Check an answer in a spare moment.

Practice sets created on iPad can be reviewed on iPhone after import. Read a question, reveal its answer and explanation, and use a short break or commute to revise.

When to use it

  • On your commute

    Read a few pages without taking out your iPad.

  • Before class

    Find your previous notes and refresh your memory.

  • Before an exam

    Check what you remember with imported questions and sticky markers.
