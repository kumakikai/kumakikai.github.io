Pairno

iPhone

In development

Track what you each pay and see who owes whom, without a joint account.

Screen from the app in development

Screenshots show the Japanese version.

Settle up as a pair, without a joint account.

Pairno is an iPhone app for two people to record what they pay and how they settle up. When one of you covers rent or an online order, record it and Pairno shows the current balance: who owes whom, and how much.

Two people connected by an invitation code share the same records. A split for each expense, receipt and order-screen reading, and a monthly reminder make settling up more reliable than keeping notes or messages.

Key features

  • Just record who paid

    Enter who paid and how much, and Pairno calculates the current balance. No deposits or withdrawals needed.

  • A split for each expense

    Go beyond 50:50 and set each person’s share in 10% steps, with the resulting amount for each of you.

  • Fill in from receipts and orders

    Read the store name and amount from a photo, a pasted image or the camera, then check them against the original before applying.

  • A monthly settle-up reminder

    On the day you choose, Pairno sends a notification only when there is a balance, so you don’t have to bring it up.

The balance at a glance, no account needed

The home screen shows the current balance and who pays whom. Record a settlement after you exchange money, or hold the button to settle the full amount.

Trace who paid what, later

History shows the carried-over balance, this month’s shared costs, settlements and the current balance by day, month or year, with a settled, partly settled or unsettled status.

When Pairno fits

  • One of you pays rent or orders

    When one person pays the full amount first and you settle each share later.

  • A joint account feels like too much

    When you want to organise shared money without opening or funding an account.

  • Bringing up money feels awkward

    When a monthly reminder on a set day makes it easier to settle up.

Compatibility and essentials

Devices
iPhone
Operating system
iOS 26.0 or later
Availability
In development
  • Both people install Pairno on their own iPhone and connect with an invitation code. Syncing records requires an internet connection.
  • Settlements are records only; the app does not send money or make payments. The app interface is currently in Japanese and amounts are in Japanese yen.
  • Receipt and other photos are stored only on the device of the person who added them and are not shared with the other person.
