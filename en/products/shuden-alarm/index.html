令和の終電アラーム

iPhone

In development

Share a screenshot showing the departure time and get an alarm before your train leaves. It rings even in silent mode, then counts down to departure on the Lock Screen.

Screen from the app in development

Screenshots show the Japanese version.

Share a screenshot, get an alarm before the last train.

令和の終電アラーム is an iPhone app that sets an alarm a chosen number of minutes before your train leaves, just from a screenshot showing its departure time. Any screen works, whether from a transit app or a map, as long as the departure time is visible. AI reads the station and departure time and works out when to ring.

The alarm rings even in silent mode. While it is set, the Lock Screen shows when it will ring and which train you are taking, and after it rings it counts down to departure. Screens and settings are kept to a minimum so you can use it in a hurry. The app is available in Japanese only.

Key features

  • Just pass a screenshot

    Choose the app from the share sheet, or use “画像を選ぶ” (Choose image) or “ペースト” (Paste) in the app. If several departures are shown, tap the train you want.

  • Choose how many minutes before

    Use the 10, 20 and 30 minute buttons or adjust in one-minute steps with − and +. Your usual number of minutes can be set in Settings.

  • Rings in silent mode

    Uses the iPhone alarm system, so it rings in silent mode and during Focus, and keeps ringing until you stop it.

  • Lock Screen view and countdown

    While set, the Lock Screen shows the ring time and train; after it rings, the time left until departure. It disappears automatically at departure.

  • Or enter the time yourself

    Without a screenshot, or if it could not be read, enter the departure time. This does not use AI, so there is no limit.

Share, choose, set

The trains found in the screenshot are listed. Tap your train, check the minutes and press the set button.

Rings even in silent mode

At the ring time the alarm sounds with the station and departure time on screen, even in silent mode, until you stop it.

After it rings, a countdown to departure

The Lock Screen shows how many minutes are left until your train leaves, so you can tell at a glance whether you will make it.

Who it’s for

  • Late nights out or at work

    When you have checked the last train and don’t want to forget when to leave.

  • No time to fiddle with alarms

    When one screenshot is easier than typing a time into the Clock app.

  • Not only the last train

    Also for the first train or your regular train.

Compatibility and essentials

Devices
iPhone
Operating system
iOS 26.0 or later
Availability
In development
  • The app is displayed in Japanese only.
  • Reading a screenshot is free once a day. Watching an ad adds up to two more reads a day. The count resets at 6:00 a.m. Setting an alarm by entering the time has no limit.
  • Screenshots are read by Google’s AI (Gemini) and sent over the internet. Check the result before setting the alarm.
  • The alarm needs alarm permission. To show it on the Lock Screen, turn on Live Activities in the iPhone Settings app.
  • Only one alarm can be kept at a time. There is no route search.
