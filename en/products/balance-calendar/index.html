Balance Calendar

iPhone

Record income and expenses as they happen. Look back at your daily finances in a calendar.

Availability
  • 🇯🇵
  • 🇺🇸

Screenshots show the Japanese version.

Record an amount now. Look back by date.

Balance Calendar is a simple way to record positive and negative amounts. Whether a day ends in a gain or a loss, keep the date and amount together so you can check them later.

Review your entries by day, month, and yearly total. Use tags to separate activities such as gambling, or rename them for everyday spending and savings records.

Key features

  • Start with plus or minus

    Choose the direction of the entry and enter an amount on a simple screen. You can also select the date.

  • See daily, monthly, and yearly totals

    Switch between dated entries, monthly balances, and a yearly total to review the figures you recorded.

  • Separate activities with tags

    Use tags to distinguish activities such as different kinds of gambling. Change tag names and icons to fit your use.

  • Open entry from a widget

    The Home Screen widget’s plus and minus buttons open the corresponding entry screen in the app, ready for an amount.

Two large buttons to start a record

Choose plus or minus, then enter an amount. The recording screen shows the date and selected tag as you log the balance.

From a day’s entries to monthly and yearly totals

The history screens show dated records and monthly or yearly totals. Follow the dates to review the amounts you have entered.

When it is useful

  • Keeping a gambling record

    Record gains and losses from your activities with a date and tag.

  • Noting spending or savings

    Keep the amounts you need as positive and negative entries without maintaining a detailed household budget.

  • Reviewing a month

    Check recorded daily figures and monthly totals instead of adding them up from memory.
