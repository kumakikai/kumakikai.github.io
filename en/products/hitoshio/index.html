HITOSHIO

iPhone / Android

Tides, weather and fishing chance at a glance. Your own points and a fishing diary in one app.

Availability
  • 🇯🇵

Screenshots show the Japanese version.

See at a glance when the fish will bite.

HITOSHIO is an iPhone and Android app that brings together tides, weather and a fishing-chance guide for each point, plus a fishing diary. The current score and the day’s best time show at a glance when fish are likely to bite. Besides public points such as fishing ports and sea-fishing facilities, you can register any spot on the map, a rocky shore or the tip of a breakwater, as your own point and see the same information there.

After a day out, pick your photos and videos and they become a timeline in shooting order, with catches and the time of each hit. Records can be shared as a tall image or a card with friends who don’t have the app. As records build up, the times you caught fish at a point are overlaid on its fishing-chance graph, and your own spots start to show their patterns.

Key features

  • TODAY while you fish

    Register one point to TODAY and check the current chance, the best time, hourly weather, wind and tide level, and how far the tide has risen or fallen, right away.

  • When next? Compare in POINT

    Line up your favourite points and compare each day’s fishing chance and best time. The number of days shown differs between the free version and HITOSHIO Plus.

  • Any spot on the map as your own point

    Line up the crosshair with “ここを登録” (Register here) and give it a name. You get the same tide, weather and fishing-chance view as for public points.

  • Tides, weather and warnings per point

    Based on the Japan Meteorological Agency’s predicted tides, see high and low tide times and the tide movement; tide movement, dawn and dusk, wind and rain give a 0–23 h fishing-chance guide as a bar chart. Apple Weather conditions, temperature, precipitation chance, wind and UV appear every 3 hours, and JMA warnings and advisories for the point’s area. HITOSHIO Plus also shows the latest sea-surface temperature, up to the previous day, in TODAY.

  • A diary of photos and videos

    Choose a cover photo, date and point, pick photos and videos, and they line up as a timeline in shooting order. Record species, count, size and the time of each hit. A record can be sent as a tall image or a card through LINE and other apps, with no coordinates or photo location data included.

  • Check from your wrist on Apple Watch

    See the current chance, the best time and the tide in the Watch app and in watch-face complications. Open the app during the best time and the Peka lamp lights up (the Peka lamp is free; the other displays need HITOSHIO Plus).

Will they bite now? The number tells you.

The point registered to TODAY shows its current fishing chance as a score. The day’s best time, weather, tide level and water temperature are on one screen (water temperature with HITOSHIO Plus).

A photo and a few words keep your fishing diary.

Under the cover photo and title, photos and videos line up in shooting order. Weather, wind and tide are recorded automatically when you save, and you can keep the time of each hit.

Every glance at your watch shows the bite time.

Check the current fishing chance and the best time on Apple Watch. Put them on the watch face as complications, and the Peka lamp lights up during the best time.

Who it’s for

  • You have your own fishing spots

    When you don’t want to look up tides and weather in separate apps for a rocky shore or breakwater that isn’t on any list.

  • You want to keep fishing records

    When you want catches and photos tied to the point so you can look back later.

  • You want to show friends easily

    When you want to share a trip with friends who don’t have the app, in a single image.

Compatibility and essentials

Devices
iPhone / Android
Operating system
iOS 26.0 or later / Android 8.0 or later
Availability
Available · App Store
  • The app interface is currently in Japanese. Tide levels, warnings and public points cover Japan.
  • Weather, wind, tides and the fishing-chance guide are estimates and do not guarantee catches or safety. Tide levels are JMA predicted values and do not reflect weather effects.
  • The basic features are free, and the free version shows ads in the diary list. HITOSHIO Plus, an auto-renewing subscription, removes ads, extends the fishing-chance and tide days and the favourites limit, and adds water temperature and the Apple Watch app (displays other than the Peka lamp). It is purchased through App Store or Google Play in-app billing.
  • Fetching weather requires an internet connection. Diaries, photos and points are stored on the device. With HITOSHIO Plus they can also sync through your own iCloud (iPhone) or Google Drive (Android).
  • The Android version uses Google Maps and does not offer Apple Maps facility search, Look Around imagery or the photo-location map in record details. The promotional images show the iPhone version and Apple Watch.
