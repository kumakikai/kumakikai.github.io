HITOSHIO

iPhone

In development

Turn any spot on the map into your own point, with tides, weather, a fishing-chance guide and a fishing diary in one app.

Your own fishing map, grown by you.

HITOSHIO is an iPhone app that brings together tides, weather and a fishing-chance guide for your own points, plus a fishing diary. Besides public points such as fishing ports and sea-fishing facilities, you can register any spot on the map, a rocky shore or the tip of a breakwater, as your own point and see the same information there.

After a day out, pick your photos and videos and they become a timeline in shooting order, with catches and the time of each hit. Records can be shared as a tall image or a card with friends who don’t have the app. As records build up, the times you caught fish at a point are overlaid on its fishing-chance graph, and your own spots start to show their patterns.

Key features

  • Any spot on the map as your own point

    Line up the crosshair with “ここを登録” (Register here) and give it a name. You get the same tide, weather and fishing-chance view as for public points.

  • Tides and a fishing-chance guide

    Based on the Japan Meteorological Agency’s predicted tides, see high and low tide times and the tide movement. Tide movement, dawn and dusk, wind and rain give a 0–23 h fishing-chance guide as a bar chart.

  • Weather, wind and warnings per point

    Apple Weather conditions, temperature, precipitation chance, wind and UV every 3 hours. JMA weather warnings and advisories appear for the point’s area.

  • “Today” while you fish

    Register one point to Today and check the current chance, the chance time and hourly weather, wind and tide level at a glance.

  • A diary of photos and videos

    Choose a cover photo, date and point, pick photos and videos, and they line up as a timeline in shooting order. Record species, count, size and the time of each hit.

  • Share as an image

    Turn a record into a tall image or a card to send through LINE or other apps. Location can be “none” or “region”, and coordinates and photo location metadata are never included.

Who it’s for

  • You have your own fishing spots

    When you don’t want to look up tides and weather in separate apps for a rocky shore or breakwater that isn’t on any list.

  • You want to keep fishing records

    When you want catches and photos tied to the point so you can look back later.

  • You want to show friends easily

    When you want to share a trip with friends who don’t have the app, in a single image.

Compatibility and essentials

Devices
iPhone
Operating system
iOS 26.0 or later
Availability
In development
  • The app interface is currently in Japanese. Tide levels, warnings and public points cover Japan.
  • Weather, wind, tides and the fishing-chance guide are estimates and do not guarantee catches or safety. Tide levels are JMA predicted values and do not reflect weather effects.
  • The basic features are free, and the free version shows ads in the diary list. HITOSHIO Plus, an auto-renewing subscription, removes ads and extends the fishing-chance and tide days and the favourites limit.
  • Fetching weather requires an internet connection. Diaries, photos and points are stored on the device.
