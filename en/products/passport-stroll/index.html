Passport Stroll

iPhone / iPad

In development

A new visa every day. Stroll the real streets of world landmarks with your fingertip and collect a stamp from every country in your passport.

Stroll the world’s landmarks with your fingertip.

Passport Stroll is a virtual travel app for iPhone and iPad that lets you walk through the scenery of world landmarks with your fingertip. Each day you open the app, a visa for the country of your choice arrives. Pick a country, pick a landmark, and real street-level scenery opens full screen: swipe to look around and tap the road to move ahead.

When you finish exploring a landmark, a visit stamp is pressed into your passport. Every country has its own stamp design, and visiting every landmark in a country earns a COMPLETE seal. The app covers 490 landmarks across the 39 countries and regions where Apple Maps offers street-level scenery: World Heritage sites and each country’s best-known sights, arranged so you only need to know the country’s name.

Key features

  • A new visa every day

    Each day you open the app, a visa for any country you like arrives, and on your first day you get visas for three. Stamp your visa page and your journey to that country begins. Even without a purchase, the daily visa lets you unlock every country in turn.

  • Stroll real streets with your fingertip

    A landmark’s scenery opens full screen. Swipe left, right, up or down to look around, tap the road in the direction you want to go, and tap × at the top right when you are done.

  • A stamp from every country, in your passport

    Every landmark you finish gets a visit stamp. Designs differ by country: Mt. Fuji and cherry blossoms for Japan, the Eiffel Tower for France. Visit every landmark in a country and a COMPLETE seal is pressed.

  • 490 landmarks across 39 countries and regions

    Choose a country and its World Heritage sites and best-known sights line up as cards. Open a card to read the landmark’s description (Japanese and English), then tap Explore to go there.

  • A Stamp Book and celebrations

    Look back on your visas and visit stamps in the Stamp Book at any time. Finish every landmark in a country and a celebration screen opens, ready to share as a single image. Finish every country and you receive a World Stroll certificate.

  • On iPad and the foldable iPhone too

    On iPad in landscape and on an open iPhone Duo, the landmark list sits on the left page and the photo and description on the right. A closed iPhone Duo shows your passport cover, and opening it begins the journey.

Who it’s for

  • You know the country, not the sights

    When you want to pick a country and see its best-known landmarks without knowing city names.

  • A little travel every day

    When you want a small daily habit of opening one new country with each visa.

  • Before a trip, or after one

    When you want to look at the streets before travelling, or walk through a landmark you have visited once more.

Compatibility and essentials

Devices
iPhone / iPad
Operating system
iOS 18.0 or later / iPadOS 18.0 or later
Availability
In development
  • The app interface is in Japanese and English and follows your device language. Landmark names and descriptions are included in both languages.
  • Viewing scenery requires an internet connection. Scenery is shown using Apple Maps data, so some places may be unavailable or may change over time.
  • The basic features are free, and the free version shows banner ads in the country and landmark lists. The one-time in-app purchase Diplomatic Passport switches your passport cover, visa pages, stamps and app icon to a diplomatic design and removes ads. As a perk, diplomats can enter every country without a visa.
  • Visas, visit records and stamps are stored on your device. There is no account registration and the app does not use your location.
  • The counts of 39 countries and regions and 490 landmarks are as of November 2026.
