SIGNAL

iPhone

Read independent voices from note, Qiita, Zenn, and more, at your own pace.

Availability
  • 🇯🇵

Screenshots show the Japanese version.

Creator posts and current topics, in one place.

SIGNAL brings together articles from sources such as note, Qiita, and Zenn, with a focus on individual writers and creators. Browse headlines and their sources without visiting each site separately.

Switch between CREATORS for creator posts, TODAY for a mix of topics, and NEWS for news coverage. Open a headline to read the original page inside the app.

Key features

  • A feed of individual voices

    Browse articles from the built-in selection of sources, including note, Qiita, Zenn, Hatena Bookmark, and DevelopersIO.

  • Choose your sources

    Turn each built-in source on or off in Settings to choose what appears in your feed.

  • Read the original article

    Open an article from its headline. Adjust article text size and choose a light, dark, or system theme.

  • A feed shaped by your responses

    Reading activity and GOOD/BAD feedback influence the order when the feed refreshes. Learning data stays on your device.

Discover a different perspective

CREATORS gathers posts from individual writers and creators. Scan headlines and sources, then choose an article that interests you.

Set the scope of your reading

Manage source switches in one settings screen, alongside the theme and article text size that suit how you read.

When SIGNAL fits

  • Catching up on technical writing

    Check posts from sources such as Qiita and Zenn without moving between their sites.

  • Looking for individual perspectives

    Find articles about a writer’s ideas and experiences alongside the news.

  • Reading on your own schedule

    Open the feed when you have time and start with a headline that interests you.
