Captions for the moment you need them.

OtoMiru turns television audio and nearby conversations into captions using the microphone on your iPhone or iPad. Read words on screen when they are difficult to hear.

Choose simple mode with large buttons, or standard mode with controls for different situations. The display can suit the person using it and the conversation at hand.

Key features

  • Start with the situation

    Choose television, conversation or group mode to start captions for what you want to hear.

  • Large text and direct controls

    Simple mode presents large television, conversation and group buttons with fewer settings to navigate.

  • A display you can share

    In conversation and group modes, change the display orientation so someone across the device can read the captions too.

Start from a large button

The simple-mode home screen provides direct buttons for television, conversations and groups, making the starting steps easy to see.

Read the words on screen

A medical appointment conversation illustrates the large captions and stop button: a screen for reading words as the conversation happens.

Useful situations

  • When television speech is hard to hear

    Place your iPhone or iPad near the television or speaker to follow its audio in captions.

  • At shops and service counters

    Read the other person’s words during an order, reception visit or other face-to-face exchange.

  • With people close to you

    Use captions as an aid in one-to-one conversations with family or friends, or in a small group.
