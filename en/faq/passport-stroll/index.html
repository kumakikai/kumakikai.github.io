Passport Stroll/FAQ

FAQ (Passport Stroll)

Updated 2026-11-13

Getting started

What is Passport Stroll?

A virtual travel app for walking through the scenery of world landmarks with your fingertip. Pick a destination country with the visa that arrives every day, and when you finish exploring a landmark, that country’s stamp is pressed into your passport. The app covers 490 landmarks across 39 countries and regions. See How to Use for the controls.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone with iOS 18 or later and iPad with iPadOS 18 or later. The app is available in English and Japanese and follows your device’s language setting.

Do I need an account?

No. Your visas, visit records and stamps are stored on your device.

Do I need an internet connection?

Yes, to view scenery and landmark photos. Your passport and Stamp Book can be viewed offline.

Visas and countries

When do visas arrive?

One visa arrives each day, and you can choose any country for it. When you first start, you can receive visas for your first three countries in a row. If you allow notifications, the app lets you know when your next visa is available. See Receiving visas.

Can I undo a visa?

No. Received visas remain even if you reset your visit stamps.

Are some countries locked without the Diplomatic Passport?

No. Without purchasing, you can unlock every country, one visa a day.

Scenery and stamps

The scenery does not appear

Scenery is shown using Apple Maps data. Check your internet connection and try again later. Some places may be unavailable or may change over time.

My stamp was not pressed

A visit stamp is pressed when you open a landmark’s scenery, explore it, and finish with ×. Browsing the list without opening the scenery does not count, and neither does a scene that could not be loaded.

How do I get the COMPLETE seal?

Visit every landmark in a country. A celebration screen opens, and a COMPLETE seal is pressed on that country in your Stamp Book.

I want to start over

Go to Settings → Reset visit stamps to delete your visit records and stamps. Received visas, diplomatic entry stamps and purchase status are kept. Deleted records cannot be recovered.

Diplomatic Passport

What does the Diplomatic Passport change?

Your passport cover, visa pages and stamps switch to a diplomatic design, the app icon changes to the diplomatic version, and ads are removed. As a perk, you can enter every country without a visa, including countries added later. In countries you have visited, you can press a diplomatic entry stamp from your Stamp Book.

Is it a subscription?

No. It is a one-time purchase. Check the price on the purchase screen.

I changed devices or reinstalled the app

Sign in with the same Apple Account and tap Settings → Restore Purchase. Visit records and stamps are stored on your device and do not move to a new device automatically (they may carry over if you restore your device from an iCloud backup).

Ads and privacy

Where are ads shown?

Banner ads appear in the country list and the landmark list. They are not shown while you view scenery, or in your passport or Stamp Book. Purchasing the Diplomatic Passport removes them.

Does the app use my location?

No. The app never gets or sends your current location. See the Privacy Policy for details.
