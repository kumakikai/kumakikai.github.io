Apps#

This site introduces KUMAKIKAI apps and publishes support information.

Uni:Note#

A handwritten note app for iPad that stays close to the feel of a paper university notebook.

Uni:Note is an iPad-only note app built for focused Apple Pencil handwriting. It helps you organize subjects and notebooks while keeping the experience close to writing on paper.

Uni:Note Pocket#

An iPhone companion app for reviewing Uni:Note backups.

Uni:Note Pocket imports backups created in Uni:Note and lets you browse them safely on iPhone. It is designed as a read-only companion focused on notebook browsing, page viewing, and memorization layer toggles.

Balance Calendar#

A record app for leaving daily profit and loss behind as facts rather than impressions.

Balance Calendar is a simple logging app for reviewing daily balances accurately. It focuses on quietly confirming what actually happened instead of relying on memory or mood.

Smokeless#

A quick logging app for tracking both smoking and the times you resisted it.

Smokeless helps you record “smoked” and “didn’t smoke” with as little friction as possible. You can review changes by day, month, and year, and estimate costs from your pack settings.

SIGNAL#

A news feed app centered on individual publishing.

SIGNAL is built around sources such as note, Qiita, Zenn, and Hatena Bookmark. It comes without ads, notifications, or login, and keeps its learning on-device.
