Privacy Policy (Passport Stroll)

Last revised 2026-11-15

1. Introduction

KUMAKIKAI, an app development brand operated by Yuya Nakamura (“we”), explains in this privacy policy how information is handled when you use Passport Stroll (“the app”) or contact us about it.

Passport Stroll lets you pick a destination with a visa that arrives every day, walk through the scenery of world landmarks with your fingertip, and collect a stamp from each country in your passport. This policy covers the iPhone and iPad app planned for distribution on the App Store.

The app has no account registration. Your visas, visit records, stamps and settings are stored on your device, and we never receive them. We do not operate any server for the app. The app does communicate with the external services described below to show scenery, ads and purchases.

2. Information we handle

Stored on your device

  • Visas: the countries of the visas you received and when you received them, the date your next visa becomes available, and how many of your first visas remain.
  • Visit records: the landmarks you finished exploring and the dates you visited, each country’s stamps and COMPLETE seal, diplomatic entry stamps, and whether a celebration has been shown.
  • Purchase status: whether you own the Diplomatic Passport.
  • Settings: notifications on or off, whether the introduction and instructions have been shown, the app icon and similar settings.

None of this is sent to us. Depending on your iPhone or iPad backup settings, it may be included in iCloud Backup or other backups.

Sent to external services

  • Scenery: to show the scenery of a landmark (Apple Maps street-level imagery) and the photos in the lists, the app sends the coordinates or place identifiers of the landmarks bundled in the app to Apple’s map services. Only landmark information is sent, never your location, visit records or visas. Fetched photos are kept in memory only while you use the app and are not saved on your device.
  • Purchases: see Purchase information.
  • Ads: see Advertising.

Notifications

The app may ask for permission to notify you when your next visa arrives. Notifications are scheduled on your device; no server or push notification service is used. You can use every feature without allowing notifications, and you can turn them off at any time in the app’s settings or in your iPhone or iPad settings.

Purchase information

The Diplomatic Passport is purchased and restored through Apple’s In-App Purchase. The app checks the signed transaction information it receives from Apple on your device to determine your purchase status.

To manage purchase records, the app sends purchase transaction information to RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.). This includes transaction details such as the product purchased, the transaction identifier, the purchase date and time, the price and currency, and the storefront country or region, together with an anonymous user ID generated automatically by the RevenueCat SDK and basic app and device information such as the app version, OS version and language. Automatic collection of device identifiers such as the advertising ID is turned off. Apple is also set up to send notifications about transactions, such as purchases and refunds, to RevenueCat. Your purchase status is determined from Apple’s transaction information, so the Diplomatic Passport still works if sending to RevenueCat fails.

We do not collect your Apple Account email address, credit card number or payment method. Purchase records are kept by Apple, and Apple’s terms and privacy policy apply to them. The RevenueCat Privacy Policy also applies to the information RevenueCat handles.

Inquiries

When you contact us, we use the content you send, your reply email address, and any device or app information you choose to provide to respond and investigate the issue. We keep this information for as long as needed for support, then delete information that is no longer needed. Records required for legal purposes are kept only to the extent needed for those purposes.

Information we do not collect

There is no account registration by email address or password. The app does not use your location, camera, microphone, photo library or contacts. It does not ask for permission to track you across other companies’ apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency), and we do not use advertising identifiers. The app has no mechanism to send usage or crash information to us. Information that Google’s systems handle in connection with ad delivery is described in Advertising.

3. How we use information

Visas, visit records and stamps are used to show your passport and Stamp Book, to show celebrations, and to determine when your next visa is available. Landmark coordinates and identifiers are used to fetch scenery and photos, purchase information to provide and restore the Diplomatic Passport, and inquiry information to respond and resolve issues.

4. Storage and management

Visas, visit records, stamps and settings are stored on your device and are not stored on any server of ours. Deleting the app deletes this information from your device. Scenery photos are not saved on your device.

Purchase records are kept by Apple and RevenueCat. We aim to delete inquiry records within one year after the inquiry is resolved.

5. External services and disclosure

We use Apple for scenery and photos (MapKit) and In-App Purchase, RevenueCat for managing purchase records, and Google for advertising through AdMob and for ad consent management (User Messaging Platform). Each company’s own policy also applies to the information it handles.

Except for outsourcing necessary to provide the service, the advertising described above, or where required by law, we do not provide information to third parties. We do not sell your visit records or visas or send them for advertising.

6. Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob to show banner ads in the country list and the landmark list. Ads are not shown while you view scenery, or in your passport or Stamp Book. After you purchase the Diplomatic Passport, ads are neither shown nor loaded.

Ads are requested as non-personalized ads. The app does not ask for tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency), and it disables Google’s collection of approximate location for ads and the linking of our own user IDs. Your visit records and visas are never included in ad requests.

In regions where consent is required by law, such as the United Kingdom, Google’s consent form is shown before ads are displayed. You can change your choices from Ad privacy settings in the app’s settings.

For ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention, Google may handle identifiers such as your device’s advertising ID, approximate location inferred from your IP address, diagnostic information about your device and ads, and information about ad views and interactions. See the Google Privacy Policy for details.

7. Managing and deleting your data

You can delete your visit records and stamps with Reset visit stamps in the app’s settings. Received visas, diplomatic entry stamps and purchase status are not reset. Deleting the app deletes all information stored on your device. Copies included in iPhone or iPad backups can be managed in your device settings.

The Diplomatic Passport is a one-time purchase, so there is nothing to cancel. When you change devices or reinstall the app, use Restore Purchase in the app’s settings.

8. Use by minors

The app does not collect your age or date of birth and has no mechanism to automatically confirm age or the consent of a legal guardian. Minors should check with a parent or other legal guardian about any consent required by law to use the app or make purchases.

9. Changes to this policy

We revise this policy when laws, app features, or information handling change. We announce the changes and their effective date in the app or on the official website, and provide advance notice of changes that materially affect users. Where consent is required by law, we obtain it before applying the new handling practices.

10. Contact

You can use the contact option below to request access, correction, restriction of use, or deletion of personal information we hold, or to ask about the operator’s details. We verify your identity only to the extent needed to identify the relevant information and respond in accordance with applicable law. If we cannot fulfil a request, we explain why.

For help with Passport Stroll, use the contact option below.

Contact by email

Send only the information needed for your enquiry. Do not send passwords or verification codes.
