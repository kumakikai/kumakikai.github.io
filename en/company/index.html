Founder

About the developer

Yuya Nakamura

Software Engineer / App Developer

Yuya Nakamura has experience in software development across embedded systems, business systems and mobile apps.

Yuya does not limit app development to a particular field. Ideas come from things Yuya finds inconvenient in everyday use or from problems mentioned by people close to Yuya. Yuya turns those ideas into apps when there seems to be a need for them.
