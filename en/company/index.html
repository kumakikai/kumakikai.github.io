Company

Everyday needs, made into apps.

KUMAKIKAI plans, develops, and maintains iPhone and iPad apps that make everyday things a little simpler.

Useful tools for everyday needs.

KUMAKIKAI plans, develops and operates apps for iPhone and iPad. Taking notes in class, reading a conversation that is hard to hear, keeping track of benefit codes: we build useful tools around specific moments in everyday life.

Our products span learning, communication support, and everyday records and organisation. We distribute them primarily through the App Store and continue to review and improve their features and usability after release.

Founder

About the developer

中村 裕也

Yuya Nakamura

Software Engineer / App Developer

Yuya Nakamura has experience in software development across embedded systems, business systems and mobile apps. At KUMAKIKAI, Yuya currently plans, develops and operates apps for iPhone and iPad.

Development starts with tools Yuya would want to use personally. For Uni:Note, advice from current students also informs the app’s development. The focus is on making tools easy to use for the people who need them, with improvements continuing after release.

What matters in our development

  • Make essential tasks clear

    We consider how screens and interactions can help people get to what they need, rather than adding features for their own sake.

  • Start with the situation

    We choose features with real situations in mind, such as opening an app during class, in conversation or on the move.

  • Keep improving after release

    We continue to improve published apps by fixing issues and reviewing how they work in everyday use.
