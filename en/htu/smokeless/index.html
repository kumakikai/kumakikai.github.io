Smokeless/How to Use

How to use (Smokeless)

Smokeless is an app for quickly recording Smoked and Avoided, then reviewing counts and money totals later.

It can be used as support for quitting smoking or as a simple log of your current smoking habits.

1. Check the first tutorial and pack settings

On first launch, after the tutorial you set:

  • Cigarettes per pack
  • Price per pack

With these values set, you can review Smoked and Avoided as money values too.

2. Record from Log

On the center Log screen, you can record the green Avoided and red Smoked buttons with one tap.

  • The green button records 1 avoided cigarette
  • The red button records 1 smoked cigarette
  • If you added multiple brands, you can switch the brand before logging
  • At the top, you can check today’s counts, money conversion, and the elapsed time since the previous record

3. Review today’s records in History

From History in the top-right corner, you can review the list of records for the day.

  • Unneeded records can be deleted
  • Right after deletion, you can restore them with Undo

4. Review by day, month, and year in Calendar

In Calendar at the bottom-left, you can check records by:

  • Day
  • Month
  • Year

For each period, you can review counts and money conversion with a number-focused view.

5. Check recent changes in Charts

In Charts, you can check trends for:

  • Week
  • Month
  • 3 Months

You can review recent changes while looking at Smoked, Avoided, and Smoked avg..

6. Use the home widget

Add the home widget to check today’s Smoked / Avoided totals from the home screen.

You can also log directly from the widget.

7. Adjust Settings

In Settings, you can mainly change:

  • Theme
  • Language
  • Savings currency
  • Cigarettes per pack
  • Price per pack
  • Brand settings (up to 4 brands)
  • Over-smoking alert
  • Remove ads
  • Restart tutorial
  • Support

Record on Apple Watch

Apple Watch support in version 1.2.0 is in review. The features below will be available after release.

Apple Watch recording is included with a Remove Ads or Extra Brands Pack purchase. Purchase or restore access in the iPhone app.

Requires Apple Watch with watchOS 9 or later and a compatible paired iPhone. Install the supporting update on both devices.

  1. Set up access

    Install the supporting update on your iPhone and Apple Watch. Open Smokeless on iPhone to confirm your Remove Ads or Extra Brands Pack purchase, then open the Watch app. If it remains locked after a purchase, restore purchases in the iPhone app’s Settings.

  2. Record one cigarette

    Tap green on the left when you wanted to smoke but did not, or red on the right when you smoked. The count increases immediately, without a confirmation screen.

  3. Check today’s counts

    Each button shows its count for today. Use Smokeless on iPhone to review past records and charts or to delete a record.

  4. Sync with iPhone

    After purchase access has first been confirmed, you can record while your iPhone is disconnected. Records are stored temporarily on your Watch and sync after reconnection. If a record has not appeared, check the connection and open both apps. Records retain the time you tapped on your Watch.
