Introduction

This guide covers picking a destination with the visa that arrives every day, walking through a landmark’s scenery with your fingertip, and collecting stamps in your passport. No account is needed.

The app runs on iPhone with iOS 18 or later and iPad with iPadOS 18 or later. It is available in English and Japanese and follows your device language. On iPhone, the tabs at the bottom are Travel, Stamp Book, Diplomat and Settings; after you buy the Diplomatic Passport, “Diplomat” becomes Passport. On iPad and on an open iPhone Duo, the same items sit in the control column at the right edge of the screen.

First launch

The first time you open the app, three pages introduce how it works. Move on with Next or by swiping, then tap Get your first visa on the last page to open your first visa. Skip at the top right does the same. You can see the introduction again later from Settings → View travel instructions again.

Receiving visas

Today’s visa

  1. On the “Your first visa has arrived” or “Today’s visa has arrived” screen, choose one destination country.
  2. Tap Apply for a visa to (country). The visa card is placed on your visa page.
  3. Tap the page to press that country’s stamp.
  4. Tap Travel to (country) to open its landmarks, or Later to go to the country list.

One visa arrives each day, and you can choose any country. On your first day you can receive visas for three countries in a row. If you close the app without receiving a visa, you can still apply that day from a card marked “No visa yet” in the country list. Once you have received the day’s visa, the app shows “A new visa arrives tomorrow.”

Visa reminders

After your third visa, the app asks whether you want a reminder for the next one. Choose Notify me to allow notifications; the app then reminds you at 9:00 a.m. the day after you receive a visa, when your next one arrives. You can turn this on or off at any time in Settings → Visa reminders.

Choosing a country

The Travel tab, “Choose a country”, lists cards for 39 countries and regions in eight areas: Asia, Western Europe, Southern Europe, Northern Europe, Central Europe, North America, Oceania and the Middle East. Each card shows the country’s name and an illustration that represents it.

  • Tap the card of a country you hold a visa for to open its landmarks.
  • Countries without a visa are dimmed and marked with a lock and “No visa yet”. Tapping one takes you to today’s visa application, or to the notice that a new visa arrives tomorrow.
  • A country whose landmarks you have all visited shows a COMPLETE stamp at the top right of its card.

Choosing a landmark and exploring

Landmark cards

The landmark list shows World Heritage sites and the country’s best-known sights as cards. Each card gives the landmark’s name and an area name that places it (for example “Tokyo · Asakusa”); World Heritage sites are marked “World Heritage”. Tap a card to open its description, and tap again to close it. Tap Explore to go to that landmark’s scenery.

How to explore

The first time you tap Explore, the app shows “How to explore”. Tap Start exploring and the scenery opens full screen.

  • Swipe to look around: swipe left, right, up or down to look around you.
  • Tap the road to move: tap the road in the direction you want to go.
  • Finish with × at top right: tap × to finish exploring, and a stamp is pressed into your passport.

Scenery is shown using Apple Maps data, so an internet connection is required. If it cannot be loaded, a short message appears and × takes you back to the list.

Collecting stamps

Visit stamps

When you open a landmark’s scenery, explore it and finish with ×, an animation presses that landmark’s visit stamp, and Back to list returns you to the landmarks. A stamped landmark shows the stamp at the top right of its card. Walking the same landmark again keeps the original date.

COMPLETE and celebrations

When you have visited every landmark in a country, a celebration screen opens after the last stamp and the COMPLETE seal is pressed. Share gives you a single image with that country’s visit stamps, and Back to List returns you. Visit every country and you receive a World Stroll certificate. If you miss a celebration, it appears the next time you open the Stamp Book.

Stamp Book

In the Stamp Book tab, switch between Visas and Visits at the top to look back on what you have collected. Visas shows the visa and date for each country you hold one for; Visits shows the number of visits per country and the stamp for each landmark. Tap a stamp to enlarge it.

Diplomatic Passport

In the Diplomat tab you can buy the Diplomatic Passport, a one-time in-app purchase. It switches your passport’s cover, visa pages, exclusive stamps, entry stamps and app icon to a diplomatic design and gives you no ads. As a perk, diplomats can enter every country without a visa, including countries added later. The price appears on the Get it for (price) button on the purchase screen.

After the purchase, the tab is renamed Passport and shows your issued passport. After changing devices or reinstalling the app, sign in with the same Apple Account and tap Settings → Restore Purchase.

In a country you have not received a visa for, once you have visited one of its landmarks you can press a diplomatic entry stamp from the Visas page of the Stamp Book.

Settings

The Settings tab contains the following.

  • Visa reminders: turn the reminder for your next visa on or off.
  • Purchases: the Diplomatic Passport’s status and Restore Purchase. In regions where ad consent is required, Ad privacy settings also appears here.
  • View travel instructions again: shows the first-launch introduction again.
  • Reset visit stamps: deletes your visit records and stamps. Visas, the Diplomatic Passport and entry stamps are kept. This cannot be undone.
  • Privacy Policy and Contact: the privacy policy and the contact point for inquiries.

Using iPad and iPhone Duo

On iPad in landscape and on an open iPhone Duo, the landmark list becomes a two-page spread: the landmark list on the left page, and the selected landmark’s photo, description and Explore button on the right. Scenery fills the whole spread. A closed iPhone Duo shows your passport cover, and opening it begins the journey. Choose Use while closed to use the same screens as a regular iPhone.
